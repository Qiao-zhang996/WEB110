My Mom's Signature Dish

Step 1

First,cut a whole chicken into small pieces and peel and chop 8-10 potatoes into chunks.Slice 10g ginger,1 whole garlic and 4 green onions into small pieces Heat 4 tea spoons oil in a pan, then add the ginger and garlic to stir-fry until fragrant，also add 1-2 star anise and a 3 small piece of cinnamon stick into the pan.

Step 2

Next, add the chicken pieces into the pan and cool until the surface turns slightly golden.Pour in 1 spoon vinegar, 2 spoons cooking vine and 3 spoons soy sauce, stir them well so the chicken is evenly coated. Then add the potatoes and mix everything together with 20 oz water into it. Cover the pan, and let it simmer sticking and to blend the flavors.wait for 30 mins to let it be well cooked until both the chicken and potatoes are soft and well done.

Step 3

Before serving, sprinkle chooped green on top for extra aroma and freshness.

Recipe Summary

Ingredient Amount Purpose Flavor
Chicken 1 Whole Chicken Main Protein Rich
Potatoes 8-10 Pieces Main Vegetable Soft
Ginger 10g Add Aroma Warm
Green Onion 4 Pieces Decoration Fresh
