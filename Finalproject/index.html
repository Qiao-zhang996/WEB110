My Mom's Signature Dish

I used to hate cooking when I was young. My mom asked me to start cooking when I was around 10 years old because my parents were farmers and always very busy. At that time, all I wanted was to watch TV and have fun. I did not understand why I had to spend time in the kitchen.

However, what my mom taught me at such a young age has stayed with me throughout my life. Now that I have moved to another country, I cook for myself almost every day. Cooking has become a way for me to feel connected to home. What I miss the most is my mom's food and her special dishes.

Her signature dish is stewed chicken with potatoes. She made it for every birthday and whenever guests visited our home. The dish always made the house smell warm and comforting. It became one of my favorite memories from childhood.

Later, I learned how to cook it and shared it with friends. They all loved the rich flavor of the chicken, potatoes, ginger, and green onions. What once felt like a simple dish has now become something very meaningful to me. It carries my mom's love and the culture she passed down.

Please share with me your favorite food. It can be something your mom or dad taught you. It can also be something you learned by yourself. Food is a beautiful way to remember family, culture, and home.
